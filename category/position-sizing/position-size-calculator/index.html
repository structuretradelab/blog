How a Position Size Calculator Works

A position size calculator asks for three inputs:

  • Account balance: How much money is in your trading account
  • Risk percentage: How much of your account you're willing to lose (usually 1%)
  • Stop loss in pips: How far your stop loss is from your entry

Then it calculates the exact position size (in lots) that keeps your risk at exactly that percentage.

📊 The Formula the Calculator Uses:
Risk Amount = Account Balance × Risk %
Position Size = Risk Amount ÷ (Stop Loss in Pips × Pip Value per Lot)
📖 Related Reading: What is a Pip in Forex? — Understanding pip value is essential for position sizing.

Manual Calculation vs Calculator

Manual Calculation (2-3 minutes):

$10,000 account × 1% = $100 risk
$100 ÷ (20 pips × $10 per pip) = 0.5 lots

Using a Calculator (10 seconds):

Enter 10000 → Enter 1 → Enter 20 → Click Calculate → 0.5 lots

⚠️ The Risk of Manual Math: When you're stressed or in a hurry, you make mistakes. A $10,000 account with a 200-pip stop instead of 20 would risk 10x too much. One error can blow your account.
