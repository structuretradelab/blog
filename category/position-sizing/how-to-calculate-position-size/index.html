The Position Size Formula

Here's the formula that every professional trader uses:

Position Size (lots) = Risk Amount ÷ (Stop Loss in Pips × Pip Value per Lot)

Where Risk Amount = Account Balance × Risk %

Let's break down each part:

  • Account Balance: Your total account equity in USD
  • Risk %: The percentage you're willing to lose (1% is standard)
  • Risk Amount: The actual dollar amount you risk ($10,000 × 1% = $100)
  • Stop Loss in Pips: The distance from entry to stop loss (e.g., 20 pips)
  • Pip Value per Lot: For USD pairs, standard lot = $10, mini lot = $1, micro lot = $0.10

Step by Step Calculation

  1. Calculate your risk amount: Account Balance × Risk %
  2. Calculate your stop loss in dollars: Stop Loss in Pips × Pip Value per Lot
  3. Divide risk amount by stop loss value: Risk Amount ÷ Stop Loss Value = Position Size in lots

Quick example: $10,000 account, 1% risk ($100), 20 pip stop loss on EUR/USD (pip value $10/lot).

$100 ÷ (20 × $10) = $100 ÷ $200 = 0.5 lots

Example 1: Small Account ($500)

Scenario: You have a $500 account. You want to trade EUR/USD with a 20 pip stop loss. You risk 1% per trade.

  • Risk amount = $500 × 1% = $5
  • Stop loss value = 20 pips × $10 per lot = $200 per lot
  • Position size = $5 ÷ $200 = 0.025 lots
📊 Result: Trade 0.025 lots = 2.5 micro lots (0.02 or 0.03 depending on broker rounding). Each pip is worth $0.25.

Example 2: Medium Account ($5,000)

Scenario: You have a $5,000 account. You want to trade GBP/USD with a 30 pip stop loss. You risk 1% per trade.

  • Risk amount = $5,000 × 1% = $50
  • Stop loss value = 30 pips × $10 per lot = $300 per lot
  • Position size = $50 ÷ $300 = 0.167 lots
📊 Result: Trade 0.17 lots (round up to nearest mini lot) = 1.7 mini lots or 17 micro lots. Each pip is worth $1.70.

Example 3: Large Account ($10,000)

Scenario: You have a $10,000 account. You want to trade USD/JPY with a 25 pip stop loss. You risk 1% per trade. USD/JPY is currently 150.50.

  • Risk amount = $10,000 × 1% = $100
  • Pip value for USD/JPY = 0.01 ÷ 150.50 × 100,000 = $6.64 per lot
  • Stop loss value = 25 pips × $6.64 = $166 per lot
  • Position size = $100 ÷ $166 = 0.60 lots
📊 Result: Trade 0.60 lots = 6 mini lots or 60 micro lots. Each pip is worth $4.

Common Calculation Mistakes

  • Forgetting pip value differences: JPY pairs have different pip values. Always check before calculating.
  • Using wrong lot size in formula: Pip value for standard lot is $10, for mini lot it's $1, for micro lot it's $0.10.
  • Not rounding down: Always round down to the nearest micro lot (0.01). Better to risk slightly less.
  • Manual calculation errors: Stress and hurry lead to mistakes. Use a calculator.
  • Ignoring account currency: This formula assumes USD account. If your account is in EUR/GBP, you need additional conversion.
