Why Fake Gurus Exist

Fake gurus exist because there's money in selling the dream of easy wealth. Not in trading — in selling courses to people who want to trade.

  • Trading is hard. It takes years to become consistently profitable.
  • People want shortcuts. They want to believe there's a secret strategy.
  • Fake gurus provide that illusion — for a price.
  • They make money selling courses, subscriptions, and signals.
  • If they were profitable traders, they wouldn't need to sell you anything.
⚠️ The Hard Truth: No consistently profitable trader spends hours making Instagram reels and TikTok videos. They spend their time trading, journaling, and improving. The gurus' full-time job is marketing — not trading.

How to Spot a Fake Guru (Red Flags)

Red Flag Why It's Suspicious
Rented luxury cars / private jets Real traders don't need to show off. They trade.
"Guaranteed profits" / "Risk-free" Trading always has risk. No one can guarantee profits.
$997+ courses "on sale" from $10,000 Fake discounts to create urgency. Real traders don't sell courses.
Screenshots with tiny position sizes but claim big profits Fake screenshots. Or they risked 50% of account (gambling).
No verifiable track record If they're so profitable, where's the MyFxBook or audited statement?
📌 Pro Tip: Ask them for a verified MyFxBook link showing 12+ months of trading history. If they can't provide it, they're lying. 99.9% cannot provide it.

The Business Model: Selling Dreams, Not Trading

Here's how fake gurus actually make money:

  1. Step 1: Post flashy content to attract followers (luxury cars, "profits")
  2. Step 2: Build a large following (appearance of credibility)
  3. Step 3: Sell a "mentorship" or "course" for $500-$5,000
  4. Step 4: Get 100 people to buy = $50,000-$500,000
  5. Step 5: Repeat with new customers
⚠️ The Math: Selling 100 courses at $997 = $99,700. They don't need to trade at all. The course is their business. Your tuition is their profit — not trading.

The Bottom Line

Here's the truth that fake gurus don't want you to know:

  • Trading is hard. It takes years of practice, journaling, and discipline.
  • There is no secret strategy. The most profitable traders use simple concepts (support/resistance, trend lines, risk management).
  • You don't need a $5,000 course. Everything you need to learn is available for free or cheap.
  • If it sounds too good to be true, it is. "Make $10,000/month with 30 minutes of work" is a lie.
