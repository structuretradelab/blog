What is a Lot in Forex?

A lot is the standardized unit of measurement for forex trades. It represents a specific number of currency units.

Quick reference:

  • Standard lot (1.0): 100,000 units
  • Mini lot (0.1): 10,000 units
  • Micro lot (0.01): 1,000 units
  • Nano lot (0.001): 100 units (not all brokers offer this)
📊 Remember: When you trade 0.01 lots of EUR/USD, you're trading 1,000 Euros. That's a micro lot — the safest starting point for beginners.

Standard Lot (1.0)

A standard lot represents 100,000 units of the base currency.

  • Pip value (USD pairs): $10 per pip
  • Margin required (1:100 leverage): $1,000
  • Best for: Large accounts ($10,000+), experienced traders

Example: Trading 1 standard lot of EUR/USD means you're controlling 100,000 Euros. Each pip movement is worth $10.

⚠️ Don't start here: A standard lot is too large for most beginners. A 10-pip loss costs $100 — that's a big hit on a small account.

Mini Lot (0.1)

A mini lot represents 10,000 units of the base currency. It's one-tenth of a standard lot.

  • Pip value (USD pairs): $1 per pip
  • Margin required (1:100 leverage): $100
  • Best for: Intermediate traders, accounts $1,000-$5,000

Example: Trading 0.1 lots of GBP/USD means you're controlling 10,000 British Pounds. Each pip movement is worth $1.

📌 When to scale up: Only use mini lots once you're consistently profitable with micro lots. Don't rush to trade larger sizes.

Micro Lot (0.01)

A micro lot represents 1,000 units of the base currency. It's one-tenth of a mini lot, and one-hundredth of a standard lot.

  • Pip value (USD pairs): $0.10 per pip
  • Margin required (1:100 leverage): $10
  • Best for: Beginners, small accounts ($100-$1,000)
📌 Recommendation: Most beginners should start with micro lots (0.01). At $0.10 per pip, you can lose 100 pips and only lose $10. This gives you room to learn.

Which Lot Size Should You Use?

Beginners (First 6-12 months)

Use micro lots (0.01). You can risk $0.10 per pip. A 20-pip loss costs $2. This lets you learn without blowing your account.

After Consistent Profitability (6-12 months)

Gradually increase to mini lots (0.1). Each pip is now $1. Your risk management must be solid before scaling up.

Professional/Full-time (2+ years profitable)

Some trades may use standard lots (1.0). But many professionals still trade mini and micro lots — consistency matters more than size.

⚠️ My Rule: Lot size is not a competition. Trading 0.01 lots profitably for a year is better than blowing a 1.0 lot account in a week. Start small. Prove yourself. Then scale.

Start Small, Stay Small, Scale Slowly

The size of your lots doesn't determine your skill as a trader. I know traders who trade micro lots for years and are consistently profitable. I know traders who trade standard lots and blow their accounts monthly.

Start with micro lots (0.01). Prove you can be profitable. Then scale up slowly — one step at a time. Your account will thank you.
