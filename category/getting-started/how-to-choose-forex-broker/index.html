How to Choose a Forex Broker: 7 Things to Look For

📅 Published: May 2026 📖 9 min read 🏷️ Category: Getting Started

When I first started trading, I had no idea how to choose a broker. I picked one because an Instagram ad promised "1000x leverage" and "bonus deposits." It was a terrible decision. The spreads were huge, withdrawals took weeks, and customer support was useless.

After testing multiple brokers, I've learned exactly what matters. Choosing the wrong broker will cost you money, time, and sanity. Choosing the right broker makes everything easier.

In this guide, I'll walk you through the 7 most important things to look for when choosing a forex broker — and the red flags to avoid.

💡 Key Takeaway: Regulation is the most important factor. Never trade with an unregulated broker. Your money is not safe.

1. Regulation — The Most Important Factor

Regulation means a government authority oversees the broker's operations, ensures client funds are segregated, and provides recourse if something goes wrong.

Trusted regulators:

  • FCA (UK) — Financial Conduct Authority. One of the strictest.
  • CySEC (Cyprus) — Cyprus Securities and Exchange Commission.
  • ASIC (Australia) — Australian Securities and Investments Commission.
  • FSA (Seychelles, SVG, etc.) — Weaker regulation, but better than none.
⚠️ Never trade with an unregulated broker. If they're not regulated, your money is not protected. They can disappear overnight. I've seen it happen to traders who ignored this rule.
📌 How to Verify: Every regulated broker displays their license number on their website. You can verify it on the regulator's official website.

2. Spreads and Commissions — Your Trading Costs

Spreads are the difference between the buy and sell price. Lower spreads = lower costs. Commissions are fixed fees per trade.

Two main account types:

  • Standard account: Wider spreads, no commission. Good for beginners, small accounts.
  • Raw account: Ultra-tight spreads, small commission per lot. Better for active traders, scalpers.
Account TypeTypical Spread (EUR/USD)CommissionBest For
Standard 0.5 — 1.5 pips $0 Beginners, small accounts, less frequent traders
Raw 0.0 — 0.5 pips $3 — $7 per lot Active traders, scalpers, larger accounts
📊 For Beginners: Start with a Standard account. Lower complexity, no commissions to track. Focus on learning, not saving a fraction of a pip.

3. Withdrawals — Speed and Reliability

Withdrawal speed separates good brokers from bad ones. The best brokers process withdrawals in minutes or hours. Bad brokers take days or weeks — and often make excuses.

What to look for:

  • 24/7 withdrawal processing — Including weekends?
  • Multiple withdrawal methods — Bank transfer, credit card, e-wallets (Skrill, Neteller, PayPal)
  • No withdrawal fees — Some brokers charge $20+ per withdrawal
  • Transparent process — Clear instructions, no hidden requirements
⚠️ Red Flag: If a broker makes withdrawals difficult or slow, run. This often means they don't have enough money to pay clients. I've seen brokers delay withdrawals for weeks before shutting down.

4. Trading Platform — MT4, MT5, or cTrader?

The platform is where you'll spend all your time. It needs to be reliable, fast, and user-friendly.

Most common platforms:

  • MT4 (MetaTrader 4): Industry standard. Great for beginners. Thousands of indicators and EAs. Recommended.
  • MT5 (MetaTrader 5): Newer version. More timeframes, more order types. Not all brokers support it.
  • cTrader: Modern interface, excellent for order execution. Popular with active traders.
  • Web/Proprietary platforms: Varies by broker. Often less reliable than MT4/MT5.
📌 Recommendation: Choose a broker that offers MT4 and MT5. It's the most widely used, most reliable, and has endless free educational resources.

5. Leverage — More Isn't Always Better

Some brokers attract beginners with insane leverage offers: "1:1000 leverage!" This is not a benefit — it's a trap.

What to look for:

  • Reasonable leverage options: 1:10, 1:20, 1:30, 1:50, 1:100
  • Ability to choose lower leverage: Good brokers let you reduce leverage
  • Negative balance protection: You shouldn't lose more than you deposit
⚠️ Avoid brokers that push high leverage as a "feature." They want you to blow your account so you deposit more. Serious brokers give you reasonable leverage options and let you choose.

6. Customer Support — Test Before You Need It

You will eventually have a problem — a withdrawal issue, a platform glitch, a question about your account. When that happens, you need responsive support.

How to test:

  • Message support with a basic question before opening an account
  • Note how long they take to respond (minutes = good, days = bad)
  • Check if they offer 24/7 live chat (essential)
  • Read reviews about withdrawal support specifically
📌 Pro Tip: The best brokers have 24/7 live chat with real humans, not bots. Test them at 2 AM on a Sunday. If no one responds, keep looking.

7. Minimum Deposit — Low vs Realistic

Many brokers offer $10 minimum deposits. While this is great for testing, be realistic about what you need to trade effectively.

Minimum deposit by experience level:

  • Demo account: $0 — mandatory for first 2-3 months
  • Absolute beginner (live): $50 — $100 — enough to learn with micro lots
  • Serious beginner: $200 — $500 — realistic for proper position sizing
  • Experienced trader: $1,000+ — reasonable capital for consistent returns
⚠️ Reality Check: A $10 account is nearly impossible to trade. Spreads alone will eat your capital. Deposit enough to trade reasonably — but never more than you can afford to lose.

Red Flags to Avoid

If you see any of these, close the tab and find another broker:

  • No regulation or offshore only — Your money is not safe
  • "Guaranteed profits" or "risk-free trading" — This is a lie
  • Bonuses on deposit — Often come with impossible trading volume requirements
  • Slow withdrawals (days or weeks) — Cash flow problems = run
  • Inactivity fees — Penalizes you for not trading enough
  • No negative balance protection — You could owe more than you deposited
  • High-pressure sales tactics — "Deposit now before this offer expires!"
⚠️ The #1 Red Flag: Any broker that makes it difficult to withdraw your money. I've seen too many traders lose everything because they ignored this warning.

Take Your Time Choosing a Broker

Don't rush this decision. Open demo accounts with 2-3 brokers. Test their platforms, test their support, test their withdrawals (on demo, obviously).

Do your research. Test before you deposit. And never trade with an unregulated broker — no matter how tempting the offer.
