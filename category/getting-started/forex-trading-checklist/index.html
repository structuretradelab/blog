Before You Start

Before you even think about opening a broker account, make sure you have these foundations:

  • You understand the basics: What is forex? How do currency pairs work? What is a pip?
  • You understand leverage: You know how leverage works and why high leverage is dangerous.
  • You understand risk management: You know the 1% rule and why it's non-negotiable.
  • You have realistic expectations: You know you won't get rich quick. Trading takes time.
  • You have money you can afford to lose: Only trade with risk capital.
⚠️ If you answered "no" to any of these, go back and review. Don't skip the foundation. It's the difference between surviving and blowing your account.

Step 1: Choose a Regulated Broker

✅ Checklist

  • Is the broker regulated? (FCA, CySEC, ASIC, FSA)
  • Does it offer the platform you want? (MT4, cTrader, etc.)
  • What are the spreads and commissions? Low spreads = lower costs.
  • How fast are withdrawals? Same-day or instant is best.
  • What is the minimum deposit? Find one that suits your budget.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • ❌ Choosing a broker with no regulation
  • ❌ Falling for "bonus" offers (often have impossible conditions)
  • ❌ Picking a broker based on marketing, not reputation

Step 2: Open a Demo Account

Do not skip this step. Trading a demo account for 2-3 months is mandatory.

✅ Checklist

  • Open a demo account with your chosen broker
  • Fund it with virtual money (usually $10,000-$100,000)
  • Trade your strategy on the demo
  • Track every trade in a trading journal
  • Be profitable for 2-3 months before going live
⚠️ Reality Check: Most beginners blow their first live account because they skipped the demo. Don't be one of them.
📖 Related Reading: Demo Trading Explained — Why you need 3 months before going live.

Step 3: Learn Your Platform

You can't trade if you don't know how to place an order. Learn your platform inside out.

✅ Checklist

  • Can you place a market order?
  • Can you set a stop loss?
  • Can you set a take profit?
  • Can you place a pending order?
  • Can you add indicators?
  • Can you draw trend lines and support/resistance?
📌 Pro Tip: Practice on your demo account until these actions become automatic. You don't want to be fumbling with buttons when real money is on the line.
📖 Related Reading: How to Use MetaTrader 4 (MT4) — A complete guide to the most popular platform.

Step 4: Create Your Trading Plan

A trading plan is your roadmap. Without it, you're gambling.

✅ Checklist

  • Which pairs do you trade? (Start with 1-2 major pairs)
  • Which timeframe do you trade? (4-hour or daily recommended)
  • What are your entry rules? (Specific and objective)
  • What is your stop loss rule? (Always use one)
  • What is your take profit rule? (Minimum 1:2 risk reward)
  • How much do you risk per trade? (1% of account)
  • What is your daily loss limit? (Stop after losing 2-3% in a day)
⚠️ The Test: Can you hand your plan to a stranger and have them execute it exactly as you would? If not, your plan isn't complete.
📖 Related Reading: No Trading Plan? You've Already Lost — Why you need a written plan.

Step 5: Define Your Strategy

Your strategy is how you find trades. Keep it simple.

✅ Checklist

  • Do you use support/resistance?
  • Do you use trend lines?
  • Do you use candlestick patterns?
  • Do you use any indicators? (Moving averages, RSI, etc.)
  • Do you use multiple timeframes?
📌 Keep It Simple: You don't need a complex strategy. Support/resistance + candlestick patterns is enough to start.
📖 Related Reading: Building a Simple Trading Strategy — Step by step guide to building your strategy.

Step 6: Open a Live Account

Once you've been profitable on demo for 2-3 months, it's time to go live.

✅ Checklist

  • Deposit an amount you can afford to lose ($100-$500 recommended)
  • Start with micro lots (0.01) — each pip is $0.10
  • Risk 1% per trade — always
  • Use your trading plan — exactly as you did on demo
  • Don't change your strategy just because it's live money
⚠️ The Reality: Your first live trades will feel different. The psychology changes with real money. Start small. Be patient. The goal is survival, not profit.
📖 Related Reading: How Much Money to Start Forex Trading — Realistic deposit amounts for beginners.

Step 7: Place Your First Trade

Here's exactly what to do when you're ready:

  1. Review your trading plan — read it before you enter
  2. Identify a trade that matches your strategy
  3. Calculate your position size using a position size calculator
  4. Set your stop loss — in the order window before clicking
  5. Set your take profit — in the order window before clicking
  6. Enter the trade — click Buy or Sell
  7. Walk away — don't stare at the screen
  8. Let the trade play out — trust your plan
📌 Remember: One trade doesn't matter. Your success is determined over hundreds of trades. Don't let this trade stress you out.
📖 Related Reading: How to Calculate Position Size — Calculate your position size before every trade.

Step 8: After Your First Trade

✅ Checklist

  • Journal the trade: Entry, exit, emotion, lesson
  • If you won: Take a break. Don't increase position size.
  • If you lost: Take a break. Review the trade. Did you follow your plan?
  • Review your plan: Did you follow it exactly?
  • Plan your next trade: Same process. Same discipline. Every time.
⚠️ After Your First Trade: Nothing changes. Win or lose, you follow the same process. One trade is meaningless. Consistency over hundreds of trades is what matters.
