What the Calculator Does

The calculator asks for three inputs and instantly tells you your position size in lots:

  • Account balance (USD): Your current trading account balance
  • Risk percentage (%): How much of your account you're willing to lose (1% is standard)
  • Stop loss (pips): The distance from your entry to your stop loss
  • Currency pair: Select from 11 pairs including EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, and Gold (XAU/USD)

It also shows your risk amount in dollars, pip value, and total stop loss value.

📊 The Formula the Calculator Uses:
Risk Amount = Account Balance × Risk %
Position Size = Risk Amount ÷ (Stop Loss in Pips × Pip Value per Lot)

Real Trading Example

Scenario: You have a $5,000 account. You want to trade GBP/JPY with a 30 pip stop loss. You risk 1% per trade.

  1. Enter account balance: 5000
  2. Enter risk percentage: 1
  3. Enter stop loss: 30
  4. Select currency pair: GBP/JPY
  5. Enter current GBP/JPY rate: 190.00 (from your trading platform)
  6. The calculator shows your position size

Result: Approximately 0.09 lots (0.9 mini lots or 9 micro lots). Your risk amount is $50 (1% of $5,000).

⚠️ Important: Always round down your position size to the nearest micro lot (0.01). It's better to risk slightly less than slightly more.
