What is an Economic Calendar?

An economic calendar shows upcoming economic reports, central bank meetings, and political events that can affect currency prices.

Popular free economic calendars:

  • Investing.com Calendar — Comprehensive, adjustable time zones

Understanding Impact Levels (Low, Medium, High)

Economic calendars color-code events by expected market impact:

Color Impact Level Typical Move (EUR/USD) Action
🟢 Green Low 5-15 pips Can trade normally, but be aware
🟡 Yellow Medium 15-30 pips Caution advised, reduce position size
🔴 Red High 30-100+ pips Avoid trading 10 min before and after
⚠️ The Rule: Red (high impact) events are dangerous. Spreads widen dramatically. Price gaps are common. Stop losses can be hit even if your analysis was correct. Avoid trading around red events.

The Most Important Economic Events

For USD (Most Important Overall)

  • NFP (Non-Farm Payrolls): First Friday of every month — biggest market mover
  • FOMC Rate Decision: 8 times per year — interest rates
  • CPI (Consumer Price Index): Monthly — inflation data
  • GDP: Quarterly — economic growth
  • Fed Chair Speeches: Throughout the year — policy hints

For EUR

  • ECB Rate Decision
  • German GDP and CPI
  • ECB Press Conference

For GBP

  • BOE Rate Decision
  • UK CPI and GDP

For JPY

  • BOJ Rate Decision
  • Tokyo CPI
📌 Pro Tip: As a beginner, focus on USD events. Most major pairs involve USD. If you understand US news, you understand most of the market.

How to Use the Calendar Daily

Morning Routine (5 minutes)

  1. Open ForexFactory calendar
  2. Check the next 24-48 hours for red (high impact) events
  3. Note the time and expected impact
  4. Plan your trading session around these events

Example Daily Plan

  • If NFP at 8:30 AM EST → Don't trade from 8:20 AM to 9:00 AM
  • If FOMC at 2:00 PM EST → Close positions before 1:30 PM
  • If no red events → Trade normally with 1% risk per trade
📌 My Routine: Every morning, I check the ForexFactory calendar. If I see a red event within my trading hours, I plan to be flat (no open positions) 10 minutes before and after. This has saved my account many times.

Best Practices for News Awareness

  • Bookmark the calendar: Make it part of your daily routine
  • Set alerts: Most calendars let you set email or calendar reminders
  • Know the consensus: Markets react to surprise (actual vs forecast), not the number itself
  • Don't chase: If you miss a trade because of news, let it go. There will always be another setup.
  • Be flat before red events: No open positions = no risk from gaps
⚠️ The Most Dangerous Time: 10 minutes before and after a red event. Spreads are widest, price gaps are common, and emotional trading peaks. Just step away.

Know What's Coming

You don't need to be a fundamental analyst. You don't need to predict news outcomes. You just need to know when news is coming and plan around it.

Bookmark the ForexFactory calendar. Check it every day. Avoid trading 10 minutes before and after red events. Do this, and you'll stop getting caught by unexpected news.
