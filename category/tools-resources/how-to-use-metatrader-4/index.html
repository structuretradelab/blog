What is MetaTrader 4 (MT4)?

MetaTrader 4 (MT4) is a trading platform developed by MetaQuotes in 2005. Despite being 20+ years old, it remains the industry standard for forex trading.

Why MT4 is still popular:

  • Stable and reliable — rarely crashes
  • Hundreds of indicators and Expert Advisors (EAs)
  • Customizable charts and layouts
  • Available on Windows, Mac (via Wine), mobile (iOS/Android), and web
  • Supported by almost every broker
📊 Where to download: Most brokers provide MT4 on their website. Download from your broker's site to ensure it's configured correctly.

Understanding the MT4 Interface

When you open MT4, you'll see several key windows. Here's what each one does:

Market Watch (Left)

Shows all available currency pairs. Right-click to add or remove pairs. Double-click a pair to open an order window.

Navigator (Below Market Watch)

Shows your indicators, Expert Advisors, and scripts. Drag and drop onto charts to apply them.

Chart Window (Center)

Where you see price charts. You can have multiple charts open in different tabs.

Terminal (Bottom)

Shows your open trades, account history, alerts, and news. This is where you'll monitor your positions.

📌 Pro Tip: You can customize the layout. Drag windows to rearrange. Save your layout via Profile → Save As.

How to Place a Trade (Market Order)

Here's how to enter a trade immediately at the current market price:

  1. In Market Watch, right-click the pair you want to trade → select New Order
  2. Or double-click the pair in Market Watch
  3. Select Market Execution (this is the default)
  4. Choose Buy (long) or Sell (short)
  5. Enter your Volume (position size in lots)
  6. Set your Stop Loss and Take Profit (optional — but highly recommended)
  7. Click Buy or Sell
⚠️ Important: Always set a stop loss before clicking Buy/Sell. MT4 lets you place orders without stops — don't do this. Enter your stop loss in the order window before submitting.

How to Set Stop Loss and Take Profit

There are two ways to set stops and targets in MT4:

Method 1: During Order Placement

In the New Order window, enter the price levels for Stop Loss and Take Profit before clicking Buy/Sell.

Method 2: After Trade is Open

Once your trade is open, go to the Terminal window → Trade tab. Right-click your open position → select Modify or Delete Order. Enter new Stop Loss or Take Profit prices.

To move a stop loss on the chart:

  • Click and drag the horizontal line on the chart (the red line for stop loss, green for take profit)
  • Release at your desired level
⚠️ Never move your stop loss further away. You can move it closer to lock in profits. But moving it away turns a small loss into a big loss.

How to Add Indicators

MT4 has dozens of built-in indicators. Here's how to add them:

  1. Click Insert → Indicators from the top menu
  2. Or click the Indicators icon in the toolbar (looks like a small chart)
  3. Select an indicator (e.g., Moving Average, RSI, MACD)
  4. Adjust the parameters (period, color, style)
  5. Click OK

Most useful indicators for beginners:

  • Moving Average (Simple) — 200 period for trend direction
  • RSI — For overbought/oversold conditions
  • MACD — For momentum and crossovers
📌 Pro Tip: Don't overload your chart with indicators. Start with 1-2. Price action (support/resistance, trend lines) is more important than most indicators.

MT4 vs MT5: Which One Should You Use?

Many brokers offer both MT4 and MT5. Here's the difference:

Feature MT4 MT5
Released 2005 2010
Timeframes 9 21
Order types 2 (Market, Pending) 4 (Market, Pending, Stop Limit)
Built-in economic calendar ❌ No ✅ Yes
Multi-asset support Forex only Forex, stocks, futures, crypto
📌 My Recommendation: As a beginner, start with MT4. It's simpler, has more educational resources, and is supported by more brokers. You can always switch to MT5 later.

Practice on Demo First

MT4 can be intimidating at first. But like any tool, it gets easier with practice. The best way to learn is to open a demo account and start clicking.

Open a demo account. Practice placing trades. Set stop losses and take profits. Add a moving average. In one week, you'll be comfortable. In one month, it will feel natural.
