What is cTrader?

cTrader is a trading platform developed by Spotware Systems, released in 2011. It was designed to be a modern alternative to MT4 — with better execution, a cleaner interface, and more advanced order types.

Key facts:

  • Available on Windows, Mac, Web, iOS, and Android
  • Known for fast, transparent execution (straight-through processing)
  • Supports advanced order types (Stop Limit, OCO, etc.)
  • Built-in economic calendar and news
  • Less common than MT4 — not all brokers offer it
📊 Who uses cTrader: Active traders, scalpers, and traders who value speed and a modern interface. Less common among beginners.

Understanding the cTrader Interface

cTrader's interface is divided into several key areas:

1. Chart Window (Center)

Where you see price charts. Cleaner than MT4. Lots of drawing tools and indicators.

2. Active Symbol Panel (Left)

Shows your open positions, pending orders, and account balance. Click to manage trades.

3. Order Panel (Right or Bottom)

Where you place trades. Shows bid/ask, lets you set stop loss and take profit before entering.

4. Market Watch (Top Left or Bottom)

Shows available symbols (currency pairs, indices, commodities).

📌 Pro Tip: cTrader is highly customizable. You can drag and drop windows, change colors, and save layouts. Spend 10 minutes setting it up the way you like.

How to Place a Trade in cTrader

Market Order (Instant Entry)

  1. In the Order Panel, select the symbol you want to trade
  2. Choose Market (instant execution)
  3. Enter your Volume (position size in lots)
  4. Set your Stop Loss and Take Profit (always do this!)
  5. Click Buy or Sell

Pending Order (Entry at Future Price)

  1. In the Order Panel, choose Limit or Stop
  2. Set your desired entry price
  3. Set your Stop Loss and Take Profit
  4. Click Place
⚠️ Important: Always set a stop loss before clicking Buy/Sell. cTrader lets you place orders without stops — don't do this. Enter your stop loss in the order panel before submitting.

Advanced Features (Stop Limit, OCO, etc.)

cTrader offers order types that MT4 doesn't have. Here's what they do:

Stop Limit Order

Combines a stop order with a limit. Once price reaches the stop level, a limit order is placed. Useful for entering breakouts with controlled slippage.

OCO (One Cancels Other)

Two orders linked together. When one executes, the other is automatically canceled. Perfect for setting entry orders on both sides of a range.

If/Then Orders

Place a pending order that only activates after another order executes. Advanced — most beginners won't need this.

📌 For Beginners: You don't need these advanced order types. Market orders and basic pending orders (Buy Limit, Sell Stop) are plenty. Ignore the advanced features until you're consistently profitable.

Should You Use cTrader or MT4?

Choose cTrader if:

  • You value a modern, clean interface
  • You want fast execution (scalping, active trading)
  • You need advanced order types (OCO, Stop Limit)
  • You don't use Expert Advisors (EAs) — cTrader's automation is less developed
  • Your broker offers cTrader (not all do)

Choose MT4 if:

  • You're a beginner (more tutorials, simpler interface)
  • You use Expert Advisors (vast library available)
  • You want universal broker support (any broker offers MT4)
  • You prefer a simpler, more straightforward platform
📌 My Recommendation: Start with MT4. It's simpler and has more educational resources. Once you're comfortable, try cTrader on a demo account. See which one you prefer. You can always switch.
